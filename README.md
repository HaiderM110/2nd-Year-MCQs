### **Q1. Velocity Selector Dynamics**
**Question:** A charged particle moves through a velocity selector where the electric field is $E$ and the magnetic field is $B$. If the particle's speed is slightly increased beyond $v = E/B$, what happens to its path?

* (A) It continues in a straight line.
* (B) It deflects in the direction of the electric force.
* **(C) It deflects in the direction of the magnetic force.**
* (D) The particle stops immediately.

> **Explanation:** Magnetic force $F_m = qvB$ depends on velocity. If $v$ increases, $F_m$ becomes stronger than the constant electric force $F_e = qE$, pulling the particle towards the magnetic deflection side.

### **Q2. Ampere's Law**
**Question:** According to Ampere's Law, if the current through a long straight wire is doubled, what happens to the magnetic field strength at a distance $r$ from the wire?

* (A) It remains the same.
* **(B) It doubles.**
* (C) It becomes four times stronger.
* (D) It is reduced by half.

> **Explanation:** Since $B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}$, the field $B$ is directly proportional to current $I$. Doubling $I$ doubles $B$.

### **Q3. Bohr's Orbital Radii**
**Question:** In the Bohr model of the hydrogen atom, what is the ratio of the radius of the second orbit ($n=2$) to the first orbit ($n=1$)?

* (A) 2:1
* **(B) 4:1**
* (C) 1:2
* (D) 8:1

> **Explanation:** Using $r_n = r_0 n^2$, the radius depends on the square of $n$. For $n=2$, it is $2^2 = 4$ times larger than for $n=1$.

### **Q4. Highest Frequency Emission**
**Question:** Which transition in the hydrogen spectrum results in the emission of a photon with the highest frequency?

* **(A) $n=2$ to $n=1$**
* (B) $n=3$ to $n=2$
* (C) $n=4$ to $n=3$
* (D) $n=100$ to $n=99$

> **Explanation:** The energy gap $\Delta E$ is largest between $n=1$ and $n=2$. Since $E = hf$, a larger energy gap produces a higher frequency.

### **Q5. Solenoid Magnetic Field**
**Question:** A solenoid with $N$ turns and length $L$ carries current $I$. If the solenoid is compressed to half its original length while keeping $N$ and $I$ constant, the magnetic field inside:

* (A) Halves.
* (B) Remains unchanged.
* **(C) Doubles.**
* (D) Becomes four times greater.

> **Explanation:** $B = \mu_0 \frac{N}{L} I$. If $L$ is halved, the turn density increases, causing $B$ to double.

### **Q6. X-Ray Production**
**Question:** What is the primary difference between 'Characteristic X-rays' and 'Continuous X-rays' (Bremsstrahlung)?

* **(A) Characteristic X-rays depend on the target material's atomic structure.**
* (B) Continuous X-rays have a higher frequency than Characteristic X-rays.
* (C) Continuous X-rays are produced by electron jumps between shells.
* (D) There is no physical difference between them.

> **Explanation:** Characteristic X-rays are "fingerprints" of the element because they come from specific inner-shell electron transitions.

### **Q7. Force Between Parallel Wires**
**Question:** Two parallel wires carry current in opposite directions. The force between them is:

* (A) Attractive.
* **(B) Repulsive.**
* (C) Zero.
* (D) Circular.

> **Explanation:** Parallel currents in opposite directions create magnetic fields that repel each other.

### **Q8. LASER: Population Inversion**
**Question:** In a LASER, 'Population Inversion' refers to a state where:

* (A) The ground state is completely empty.
* **(B) More atoms are in the excited state than the ground state.**
* (C) Atoms move faster than the speed of light.
* (D) All electrons have the same spin.

> **Explanation:** For stimulated emission to dominate, we must have more atoms in the higher (excited) state than in the lower (ground) state.
